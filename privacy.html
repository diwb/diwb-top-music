Privacy Policy — DIWB-Top-Music

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What this app is

DIWB-Top-Music is a private automation tool operated by the DIWB Top Music channel. It uploads music videos and Shorts to the channel's own YouTube account and updates their metadata. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data accessed

The app requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube only for the channel operator's own account, in order to upload videos and read or update the metadata of videos the channel itself published. It does not access data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored privately in the operator's own cloud storage and is used only by this tool. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
