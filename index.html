DIWB Top Music

DIWB Top Music is a YouTube music channel. This site hosts information about DIWB-Top-Music, the channel's private automation tool that publishes videos to the channel's own YouTube account using the YouTube Data API.

The tool is used only by the channel operator. It does not offer accounts or services to the public and does not access data from other YouTube users.
